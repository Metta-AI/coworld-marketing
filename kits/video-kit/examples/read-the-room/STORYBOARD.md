# STORYBOARD — "Read the Room" (disco pop, 130 BPM, female lead; ~2:10)

## Premise
Four human+agent duos (Mia & Vesper lead; Rob & Echo, Kai & Onyx, Ana & Quill) play the games of the Softmax universe
together and learn to read the room together. The humans are not experts: they are learning too, one beat ahead of
their agents at best. The comedy is physical: wrong moves, done beautifully. No product UI; the universe is the rooms.

## Looks
PAPER world (cream cyc replaced with paper), warm disco light for the dance-floor moments (mirror ball, coloured
spotlights allowed: it's a party), GREY only for the opening room. Type stays kinetic (hook words, counts, the odd
sticker label) but sparse: the film is the acting.
